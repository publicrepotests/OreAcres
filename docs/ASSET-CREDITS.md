## OpenGameArt CC0 Music

Orehaven streams three loopable tracks after the player enables audio and crossfades them across exploration, dungeon, and combat states.

- `orehaven-field.ogg`: "Fairy Adventure" by MintoDog, CC0. [Source](https://opengameart.org/content/fairy-adventure)
- `orehaven-dungeon.ogg`: "Dark Cavern Ambient" by Paul Wortmann, CC0. [Source](https://opengameart.org/content/dark-cavern-ambient)
- `orehaven-battle.ogg`: "Chiptune Battle Music" by pmiller, CC0. [Source](https://opengameart.org/content/chiptune-battle-music)
