# Ore Acres LPC Armor Credits

The animated armor layers in this directory come from the free and open-source
[Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator).
Ore Acres uses the OGA-BY 3.0 option offered by the upstream metadata for these
assets. The files are redistributed as animation layers and tinted at runtime.
