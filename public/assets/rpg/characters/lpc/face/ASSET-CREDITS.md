# Ore Acres LPC Face Credits

The animated face-expression layers in this directory come from the free and
open-source [Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator),
revision `0f898bb675a1abe16ce430e82e3bf9daed278690`.

- Local `neutral/` maps to upstream `head/faces/male/neutral/`.
- Local `determined/` maps to upstream `head/faces/male/anger/`.
- Local `cheerful/` maps to upstream `head/faces/male/happy/`.
- Local `wide-eyed/` maps to upstream `head/faces/male/shock/`.
- Artists: JaidynReiman, ElizaWy, Stephen Challener (Redshrike).
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/ulpc-expanded-expressions
- License selected from upstream metadata: OGA-BY 3.0.

The files remain separate animation layers and are not AI-generated. No paid
asset or service was used.
